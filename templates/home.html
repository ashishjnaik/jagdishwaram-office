01·The Case

For over a decade, court-affirmed rights to land access have been recognised in writing and denied in practice — by every institution this family approached for relief. This platform documents that gap precisely: every order, every filing, every day of non-compliance, with the rigour that institutions count on citizens not having.

03·Jagdishwaram Foundation
Vision
शून्यात जन्मलो, शून्यातून उभरलो शून्याकडे प्रवास सर्वांचाच; "प्रवाह" माझा मीच बनलो. "अशाश्वत" सत्तेचे भय दाखविणाऱ्यांनो ऐका, "शाश्वत" मृत्यूचे भय मी, जिवंतपणी प्यायलो. "न्याय/अन्याय" आता शब्दमात्र झाले, "परिवर्तन" हेची एकमेव ध्येयमात्र बनले.
— आशिष जगदिश नाईक
Mission
  • Equip Replicable tools that help citizens navigate, document, and demand accountability from government authorities
  • Expose Court-order non-compliance, RTI suppression, and systemic delays converted into measurable, permanent public data
  • Educate AI literacy for underprivileged children; self-capability for citizens navigating bureaucratic systems
