For over a decade, court-affirmed rights to land access have been recognised in writing and denied in practice — by every institution this family approached for relief. This platform exists to document that gap precisely: every order, every filing, every day of non-compliance, with the rigour that institutions count on citizens not having. The tools built here turn that rigour into permanent public record — for this case, and for every citizen who has ever waited past a legal deadline that was never honoured.

About Jagdishwaram Foundation

Born out of a decade-old struggle against the systemic exploitation of fundamental rights

Vision
शून्यात जन्मलो, शून्यातून उभरलो शून्याकडे प्रवास सर्वांचाच; "प्रवाह" माझा मीच बनलो. "अशाश्वत" सत्तेचे भय दाखविणाऱ्यांनो ऐका, "शाश्वत" मृत्यूचे भय मी, जिवंतपणी प्यायलो. "न्याय/अन्याय" आता शब्दमात्र झाले, "परिवर्तन" हेची एकमेव ध्येयमात्र बनले.
— आशिष जगदिश नाईक
Mission
  • Build replicable citizen tools and methods that empower individuals to navigate, document, and demand accountability from government authorities
  • Make administrative and judicial accountability visible — turning systemic inaction, court-order non-compliance, and RTI suppression into measurable public data
  • AI Literacy & Outreach
    • Coaching underprivileged school children in AI fundamentals and digital tools
    • Enabling those navigating bureaucratic systems to become self-capable and self-reliant
    • Contributing knowledge and lived experience toward broader Social Cause
