Generate Dynamic QR

Your digital trackable record for any submission to an authority

How This Works

Purpose

Create a permanent, publicly verifiable digital record of your physical submission.

How to Use

  1. Fill the form below
  2. Click "Generate QR Code"
  3. Download and embed in your physical letter
  4. Submit within 3 days (recommended best practice)
Important Note on Dates
The system records QR generation date. For tracking purposes we treat "QR Generation Date + 3 days" as the assumed physical submission date.
