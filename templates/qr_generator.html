How This Works

Purpose
Create a permanent, publicly verifiable digital record of your physical submission. Every scan becomes immutable proof of receipt and action (or inaction).
How to Use
  1. Fill the form with your submission details
  2. Click "Generate QR"
  3. Download the QR and embed it in your physical letter
  4. Submit within 3 days (recommended best practice)
  5. Anyone can scan or search the QR ID to see full history
Important Note on Dates
The system records the QR generation date. For tracking purposes we treat "QR Generation Date + 3 days" as the assumed physical submission date (we do not yet capture the exact submission date). This is a recommended best practice, not a strict rule.
This tool exists because institutional silence has become normalized. Use it responsibly.
