How This Works

Purpose
Create a permanent, publicly verifiable digital record of your physical submission.

How to Use
1. Fill the form
2. Click Generate QR
3. Download and embed in your physical letter
4. Submit within 3 days

Important Note on Dates
The system records QR generation date. For tracking we treat “QR Generation Date + 3 days” as the assumed physical submission date.
